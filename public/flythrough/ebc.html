Everest Base Camp

Khumbu, Nepal

5,364 m, nine days on foot from Lukla and back

The place

On the Khumbu Glacier, at the foot of the icefall, where expedition teams camp each spring

The rock

A painted boulder that says EVEREST BASE CAMP, and the end of the trail for trekkers

The view

Everest, 8,849 m, with Lhotse and Nuptse beside it, seen from above

The Sherpas

They fix the ropes, carry the loads and open the route every season. They were the real strength of this trip.
